Privacy Policy – English for PAUD

Last updated: July 31, 2025

Developed by: Nurdin Dev

1. Introduction

English for PAUD is an educational mobile application designed to help young children learn basic English through interactive audio, images, and simple activities.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

Currently, we do not collect, store, or share any personal information from users.

The app can be used entirely without creating an account, without internet access, and without requiring access to your device's camera, location, contacts, or storage.

3. Permissions

This app does not request any device permissions.

If in the future access to specific features is required (such as saving learning progress), users will be clearly notified, and this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.

4. Children's Privacy

We recognize the importance of protecting children's privacy. This app is designed specifically for children under the age of 13 and does not contain advertisements, does not offer in-app purchases, and does not collect any personally identifiable information.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time as the app evolves. If significant changes are made, users will be informed via an in-app notification or app update.

The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest revision.
